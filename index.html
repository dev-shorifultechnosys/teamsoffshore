How it works

From role brief to working team.

A clear four-step model keeps responsibilities visible and makes the move offshore easier to understand.

  1. 01

    Collaborate

    Define the roles, goals, coverage, systems and preferred ways of working.

  2. 02

    Recruit

    Source, screen and shortlist professionals around your agreed criteria.

  3. 03

    Operate

    Your selected people work with your business day to day as dedicated team members.

  4. 04

    Support

    TeamsOffshore supports the operating layer around the team for the long term.
